## 1. Overview
Curriculum Core ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. Curriculum Core is an offline-first, non-AI scientific calculator and study assistant designed for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use our application.

## 2. Information We Collect and Process
Curriculum Core is designed with a privacy-first approach:
- **Offline Data Storage:** All user calculations, custom plugins, notebooks, and history generated within Curriculum Core are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your notebook content or mathematical calculations on external servers.
- **Device & Usage Identifiers (AdMob):** The free version of Curriculum Core uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (adaptive banners, interstitials, and rewarded videos). Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, and coarse location data to serve ads, prevent fraud, and perform analytics.
- **Purchase Data (Google Play Billing):** When you purchase or renew a Curriculum Core Pro subscription, transactions are processed directly by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your credit card details or financial information. We only receive verification tokens from Google Play to confirm your subscription status.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Curriculum Core integrates the following third-party SDKs, which handle data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:
- **Google AdMob:** Serves advertisements. 
  - Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google Play Services / Google Play Billing:** Manages application distribution, licencing, and subscription purchases.
  - Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Children's Privacy
Curriculum Core does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Any advertising served through AdMob complies with Google Play's Families Policy and certified network requirements where applicable.

## 5. Your Choices & Data Control
- **Ad Customization:** You can reset your device’s Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads via your Android device settings (`Settings > Google > Ads`).
- **Data Removal:** Deleting local app data or uninstalling Curriculum Core completely removes all locally stored notebooks, calculations, and local settings from your device.
